Common Questions

How long are the coaching contracts?
We typically ask for a minimum 6-month commitment for 1-on-1 coaching to ensure we have enough runway to implement systems and see measurable ROI. Real change requires consistent execution over time.
Are the mastermind cohorts virtual or in-person?
The monthly masterminds are held virtually via Zoom for convenience. However, cohort members get priority access and exclusive discounts to our bi-annual in-person retreats held in major cities.
What makes this different from other coaching?
We don't focus on simple motivation or "rah-rah" energy. We focus on structural business engineering. If you need a cheerleader, we aren't for you. If you need a strategic partner to build scalable systems and hold you accountable to the metrics that matter, you're in the right place.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Absolutely. Many of our clients start in a Cohort to build their foundational systems, and seamlessly upgrade to Elite 1-on-1 when they are ready for personalized, rapid scaling.
